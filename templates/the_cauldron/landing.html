    {% trans "The Method" %}

    {% trans "How the difficulty rises" %}

    {% blocktrans trimmed %}The Forge runs an autoregulated double-progression engine (APRE-style): the last working set of every exercise is an all-out test, and its result decides your next session. Two levers turn — climb the ladder, or add load — and the engine picks the right one for the movement and the gear you own.{% endblocktrans %}

    {% trans "Lever 1 — Difficulty" %}

    {% trans "Climb the movement ladder" %}

    {% blocktrans trimmed %}For pure bodyweight work, harder is the progression. Hit the top of your rep range for two sessions running and the next rung is earned.{% endblocktrans %}

    {% blocktrans trimmed %}But it never jumps for you: the unlock is parked as a celebration you Claim or hold. Fall below the range and the Forge regresses you a rung to rebuild — no grinding reps you can't do with form.{% endblocktrans %}

    {% trans "Lever 2 — Load" %}

    {% trans "Add the smallest real increment" %}

    {% blocktrans trimmed %}When you own loadable gear — dumbbells, a barbell, bands — the Forge switches to load progression: beat the top of the range and it adds the smallest weight you actually have, then resets your reps.{% endblocktrans %}

    {% blocktrans trimmed %}This solves the hardest problem in bodyweight training — overloading the legs and hips — and de-loads automatically when a set comes up short.{% endblocktrans %}

    {% trans "Measured, not guessed" %}

    {% trans "Objective from the first rep" %}

    {% blocktrans trimmed %}Both levers are driven by a real AMRAP count, not a subjective “how hard was that?” Beginners misjudge proximity to failure by 4–5 reps, so the Forge measures the number and adapts from fact.{% endblocktrans %}

    {% blocktrans trimmed %}Blocked a move for injury or preference? It's swapped for the closest equal-or-easier substitute in the same pattern — the ladder bends around you.{% endblocktrans %}

    {% trans "Recovery, dialled in" %}

    {% trans "Rest matched to the work" %}

    {% blocktrans trimmed %}Rest isn't an afterthought. Heavy, low-rep and loaded compounds get ~2½ minutes; hypertrophy ranges ~2; endurance sets ~75 seconds; isometric holds ~60 — each grounded in the training literature.{% endblocktrans %}

    {% blocktrans trimmed %}The result: progress you can sustain, on a schedule your body can actually recover from.{% endblocktrans %}
